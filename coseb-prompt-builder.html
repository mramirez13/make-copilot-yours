The prompt formula

Great prompts have four ingredients

Tell Copilot who the work is for, what you want to accomplish, which information to use, and what a helpful answer looks like.

C

Context

What's the situation?

Your role, the audience, and why this work matters.

G

Goal

What do you want to get done?

An invitation, a briefing, a summary, or a practical plan.

S

Source

What should Copilot work from?

The brief, notes, or approved material you actually provide.

E

Expectations

What does good look like?

The format, tone, length, and how to handle missing facts.
